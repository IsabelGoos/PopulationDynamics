# Macroscopic Population Models

In this folder, I explore population dynamics through two classical mathematical models: the **Malthusian exponential model** and the **Verhulst logistic model**.

At this stage, I consider a macroscopic representation of the population, treating it as a homogeneous system described by its total size over time. Individual characteristics and population structure, such as age distribution, are initially neglected to establish a simplified mathematical framework.

The focus is on understanding how unrestricted exponential growth compares with growth constrained by environmental limitations.
